Gone Campin'

Some people don't come back._

A Wilderness Survival Game

Begin Survival

About the Game

Gone Campin' drops you alone into one of several hostile biomes — dense boreal forest, scorching desert, frozen tundra, or fog-choked swampland — with nothing but your wits and whatever you can scavenge. Every decision costs time, every night is more dangerous than the last, and the wilderness does not care whether you make it home.


Manage five survival stats, craft tools and shelter from raw materials, and navigate random events that can flip a comfortable camp into a desperate fight for your life. Your only way out: build a Signal Fire large enough to be seen from the sky.

How to Play

🌄

Day & Night Phases

Each day is split into three phases: Morning, Afternoon, and Night. Conditions change across phases — temperatures drop at night, predators grow bolder, and certain resources only appear at specific times.

⚡

Action Points

You have a limited pool of Action Points (AP) each phase. Every activity — foraging, building, resting, exploring — costs AP. Spend them wisely; running dry in the wrong place can be fatal.

📊

Your 5 Stats

  • ❤️ Health — your life. Reaches zero and it's over.
  • 🍖 Hunger — find food or starve.
  • 💧 Thirst — dehydration kills faster than hunger.
  • ⚡ Energy — low energy reduces AP next phase.
  • 🔥 Warmth — hypothermia is a slow, quiet death.
🪓

Crafting System

Combine raw materials found in the wild to craft tools, shelter, traps, and medicine. Better tools unlock faster gathering; better shelter guards your Warmth through the night.

🎲

Random Events

Each phase can trigger a random event — a rainstorm that refills your water but chills you to the bone, an animal encounter, a strange discovery, or a streak of unexpected good fortune. Stay adaptable.

🔥

Win Condition

Survival is not the goal — rescue is. Gather enough materials to construct a Signal Fire at your camp. Light it, keep it burning, and hope someone is watching the sky.

Good luck. You'll need it.
